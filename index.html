Questions

Is it a candle?

No. There is no flame, no wax and no smoke. A small flameless LED tealight sits inside and the stone does the rest. It is designed for an LED and nothing else; the warning is embossed on the inside of every piece where you will never see it unless you look.

What light does it take, and is one included?

Any flameless LED tealight 37.5–38 mm across, the size sold everywhere in packs of 12 or 24. One comes in the box, and it is the exact size to buy next time. Everything about the light.

How long does the light last?

Around a hundred hours of light from one cell. Lit every evening, that is a few months; then a fresh cell or a fresh light. A rechargeable set fits the same seat if you would rather never buy a cell.

Can it go outside?

For an evening, yes: a step, a garden table, a tent. Rain runs off it and the light stays lit, and a lit stone in snow is the best it ever looks. It is not made to live outdoors through a season, and it must stay out of direct sun. Heat is the only thing that can hurt it: never above 50 °C / 120 °F, so not a hot car and not a radiator.

Is it safe around children and pets?

Nothing burns and nothing gets hot, so it can go on a bookshelf, a windowsill, beside a bed or into a child's room. The one thing to keep from small children is the tealight itself, because like every small light it runs on a button cell.

Why does mine look different from the photographs?

Because no two are the same. The lichen, the cracks and the fractures on yours are yours alone. The photographs show one piece; you are holding another.

Does it come gift-ready?

Yes. Tissue, the seal, a care card, and nothing to assemble: the light drops in from the top and the base is already on. It can be handed over as it arrives.

How do I clean it?

A dry brush, or a barely damp cloth. No solvents, no dishwasher.

Do you ship to the United States?

Yes. Everything ships tracked from Vancouver Island, to Canada and the US, with the customs form filled in properly so nothing waits at the border.

What if it arrives damaged?

Photograph the box and the piece the day it arrives and write to hello@arpilo.ca. We will sort it out.

Can I order a custom piece, with a name or a date in it?

Not yet. Custom pieces come later this winter; write and we will keep your name.

Do you sell to shops?

A small number, on Vancouver Island and in BC. Write for a line sheet.

About

Developed and made in Victoria, at the bottom of Vancouver Island.

A harbour city where it rains from October to March and the evenings start at four. Half the year is dark by dinner, which is why a small light on a sill matters here, and why every piece is tested lights-off before it is packed.
