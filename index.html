Some things only show in the dark

From this coast

Everything Arpilo makes begins at the edge of British Columbia, where the Pacific meets stone older than the sea beside it. Nature keeps most of what it makes out of sight. We make it quiet by day, and let the dark give up what is inside.

Lichen

Walk any shore on this coast and the rocks are written on: a black crust where the spray reaches, orange and gold rosettes above it, grey-green maps that grow a fraction of a millimetre a year. Each is two lives in one, a fungus and an alga that agreed to share. Lichen keeps its colony in small cells divided by dark lines. Ours does the same, and shows them only when the light is behind it.

Ammonite

The same shores remember an older sea. The sandstones of Hornby Island and the east coast of Vancouver Island settled on a sea floor seventy million years ago, and they still give up ammonites, the coiled shells of animals that swam here, turned to stone. Our slab holds one the way the rock does: a faint impression by day and, by night, the whole shell, chamber by chamber.

Nodule

Some of those shells were sealed inside nodules, rounded stones that formed around them on the ancient sea floor and kept them whole. Split one on the beach and the ammonite is there, where it has waited all along. Our nodule is that moment, held: a stone like any other until the light inside finds what it carries.

Lichen, ammonite, nodule and arbutus are in the lab now. They join the catalogue as each one is ready.

Questions

Is it a candle?

No. No flame, no wax, no smoke. The logs and the campfire take a flameless LED tealight; the lanterns have fairy lights inside.

Is a light included?

Yes. Logs and the campfire come with a warm LED tealight, cell fitted. Lanterns come with a fairy string and remote. Everything about the light.

Can it go outside?

No. Indoors only, never above 50 °C / 120 °F: not in direct sun, a hot car or on a radiator.

Is it safe around children and pets?

It is decor, not a toy, and has small parts. Tealights run on a coin cell: keep new and used cells away from children.

Why does mine look different from the photographs?

A phone sees light differently from your eye. Every glow photograph here is a real piece, lit with the room lights off, cropped, nothing else.

Do you ship to the United States?

Not yet. Within Canada only.

About

Arpilo is a small studio in Victoria, on the southern tip of Vancouver Island. On this coast it rains from October to March and the evenings start at four. Half the year is dark by dinner, which is why a small light matters here.

We design and make our own pieces here, and we choose every stone and bracelet we sell by hand, photographing each one in the same light so what you see is what arrives. Every piece is tested lights-off before it is packed.
