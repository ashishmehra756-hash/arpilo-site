Questions

How do the ornaments hang?

Each one arrives on its own thread, ready to hang: on the tree, a door, a cabinet knob or a window.

What do the dishes hold?

Rings, earrings and bracelets, keys or a few small stones.

Why does mine look different from the pictures?

A phone sees colour differently from your eye. Our photographs are our own, of real pieces; the newest ornament designs are shown as drawings until their photographs are in.

Do you ship to the United States?

Not yet. Within Canada only.

About

Arpilo is a small studio in Victoria, on the southern tip of Vancouver Island. On this coast it rains from October to March and the evenings start at four. Half the year is dark by dinner, which is why a small light matters here.

We design and make our own ornaments, dishes and trays here, and we choose every stone and bracelet we sell by hand and photograph each one ourselves. Every piece is checked before it is packed.
