The light.

Every Arpilo piece is lit by an ordinary flameless LED tealight. This page is everything about it.

What comes in the box

With the Lichen, two warm flameless tealights, cells installed. With the Lantern, a warm copper fairy string. Any other flameless LED tealight fits the same seat. No open flame, ever.

Size
Flameless LED tealights, coin-cell or rechargeable. The seat is made to take them, not one brand.
Cell
A CR2032 coin cell, already installed, with a pull-tab.
Run time
Makers quote anything from a day of steady light to a hundred hours from one cell, and we do not repeat a number we have not measured. The card in the box states the figure for the light we ship.

Buying more

Any flameless LED tealight. They are sold in packs of 12 or 24 at hardware stores, dollar stores and online for well under a dollar each. Warm white looks like a flame; cool white looks like a fridge. Get warm.

If you want longer nights, a rechargeable set (six lights, one charging tray, a remote) drops into the same seat. They are a little dimmer than a fresh coin-cell light, and the stone was calibrated for the dim end, so it still works.

Only ever an LED

Never a flame. A candle will ruin it. The warning is embossed on the inside of every piece, where you will never see it unless you look.

Changing it

It lifts out. The night light loads from the top; the slab and the nodule lift off their bases. Nothing unscrews, nothing unclips, and there is nothing to break.
