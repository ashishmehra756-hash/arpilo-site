The walls

Every maker hits the same walls. We hit them too.

"Where do I even start?"

Business name, insurance, GST, what's allowed. One session and a checklist in plain words.

"The fees eat my margin."

Etsy takes roughly 11-15% of a sale, and more with its offsite ads. Our shelf takes no commission and no fee per sale: one flat monthly fee, and buyers pay you directly.

"My photos don't do it justice."

Phone grain, wrong light, no sense of size. Real photos to one standard: daylight, lit, and something beside it for scale. No AI fakes.

"Nobody can find me."

A shop with no traffic is a shop in a basement. We market the whole shelf on Pinterest, Instagram, Google and at local events.

"Shipping is expensive and confusing."

Which carrier, which box, which label. A packing and shipping setup that works from day one, plus local delivery in Greater Victoria.

"I don't know what to charge."

Too low and you work for free; too high and nobody buys. A pricing review against what people really pay for pieces like yours.

"Markets cost too much to try."

Booth fees, juries, whole weekends standing. An online shelf for CA$30 a month, less than one market weekend, and you can stop any month.

"The tech is overwhelming."

Checkouts, product feeds, search settings. No website needed: your page on the shelf, with a link to share anywhere. Buyers buy through your Etsy, your own site, a payment link, or by message. Send photos from your phone; we do the rest.

How it works

Four steps, all online. No meetings needed.

  1. Choose your path: the know-how, the shelf, single services, or the full launch.
  2. Send your product details and photos straight from your phone. For photo packages, we tell you where to send the pieces.
  3. We set it up and check every listing against the same shelf standard.
  4. It goes live. You make; we market the shelf.

Our promises

Plain rules, no small print.

  • You own everything. Your accounts, shop and photos are in your name. We never ask for your passwords; we work through each platform's team access.
  • We sell work, never results. No promised followers or sales, ever.
  • We teach only what we've done. Every service here is something we built for our own studio first.
  • Real photos only. What a buyer sees is what they get.
  • We hold the standard, not you. You don't have to prove yourself. Every listing meets the same shelf standard (real photos, true descriptions, fair price), and we help get it there.
  • We're makers too. Our own pieces sit on the same shelf, with the same rules and fair placement.

Your side of it

What stays your responsibility.

We tell you plainly which rules apply to what you make. Taking care of them stays with you.

  • Your business: registering a business name, keeping records, and GST once your sales pass CA$30,000 a year.
  • Your product's safety and labels: some things have their own Canadian rules, for example children's items, candles, cosmetics and textiles.
  • Copyright and trademarks: your designs must be yours. No other brands' logos, characters or artwork.
  • Insurance: we explain what product liability insurance is and when it makes sense; the choice is yours.
  • Your sales: buyers pay you directly, so taking payment, delivery and returns are yours to run, the same as at a market table. We show your work; we never handle the money.
  • Makers in Canada: the shelf is for things made by makers who live and make here.

This is general information to point you the right way, not legal advice.
